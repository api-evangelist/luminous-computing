# Luminous Computing

> Mountain View, California silicon-photonics company (branded "Luminous") founded in 2018 by Marcus Gomez (CEO), Mitchell Nahmias (CTO) and Michael Gao, building an optical AI supercomputer that moves data with light instead of electricity to eliminate the interconnect bottleneck between compute and memory. The architecture derives from Nahmias' neuromorphic-photonics research at Princeton. Luminous emerged from stealth in March 2022 with a $105M Series A backed by Bill Gates, Gigafund, 8090 Partners, Neo, Third Kind Venture Capital, Alumni Ventures, Strawberry Creek Ventures, Horsley Bridge and Modern Venture Partners; total disclosed funding is roughly $123-126M across three rounds. Silicon-photonics veteran Michael Hochberg (Luxtera, Elenion) joined as president of engineering and operations in 2023.
